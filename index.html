Which areas do you cover?

Is the home loan calculator a bank quotation?

No. It is an indicative monthly repayment estimate using the price, down payment, annual interest rate and tenure you enter. It excludes legal fees, stamp duty, insurance and other purchase costs. Actual rates and approval depend on the lender.

Use any property enquiry button or the contact form to open a pre-filled WhatsApp message to Kelvin. Send the message in WhatsApp to arrange a suitable viewing time.
